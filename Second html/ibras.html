Contact sport and non contact sport

Contact sport

A contact sport is any sport where physical contact between players is
either an acceptable, expected, or mandatory part of gameplay.

Example of contact sport

  • Football
  • Basketball
  • Hockey
  • Rugby
  • soccer

Non contact sport

A non-contact sport is any sport where physical contact between opposing players is
either strictly prohibited by the rules or impossible due to the nature of the game.

Example of non-contact sport

  • Swimming
  • Tennis
  • Achery
  • Golf
  • Bowling
Impotant of contact sport
  1. It improves physical fitness
  2. It develops teamwork
  3. It builds courage
  4. It develops coordination
Impotant of non-contact sport
  1. It improves physical health
  2. It reduces risk of injury
  3. It improves concentration
  4. It promotes discipline
